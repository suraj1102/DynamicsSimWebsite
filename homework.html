Homework

Problems, solutions and the grading policy

Homeworks are worth 50% of your grade and are due Tuesday 11 PM every week. A late submission is worth zero points.

You are expected to put roughly 4.5 hours a week into these problems, and to evaluate each other’s effort claims — see Self-Policing in the course outline.

You can also checkout Jia’s Homework Solutions and Tanay’s Homework Solutions from the Plaksha 2025 version of this course. The same problems have been prescribed to the Plaksha 2026, some changes maybe done as the course continues.
